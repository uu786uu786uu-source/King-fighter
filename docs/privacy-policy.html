Guftin Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guftin ("Guftin", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Guftin mobile messaging application and related services (the "Service").

Guftin is a messaging and calling service. Information you choose to put in your profile, send in a chat, share in a group, or publish as a status may be visible to the people or contacts you choose, and to the service providers needed to operate the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: email address, account identifier, and basic account information returned by Google when you choose Google sign-in. We use this information to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information: your Guftin username/ID, display name, profile photo, and any "about" text you choose to add.
  • Contacts and conversations: Guftin users you search for by username and add, contact relationships, direct and group membership, messages, and related conversation information.
  • Shared content: photos, videos, voice messages, files, status posts, and status views or likes that you choose to send or share through the Service.
  • Calls: information needed to set up and operate voice/video calls, such as call participants and connection or session information. Audio and video are transmitted through our calling technology provider.
  • Notifications: push-notification token and settings for a device when you enable notifications, so the Service can deliver message, call, or status alerts.
  • Technical and advertising information: app/device information, diagnostics, and ad-related identifiers or interaction information that may be processed by the advertising SDK used to display ads.

2. Permissions and device access

Guftin may ask for camera and microphone access when you use camera or calling features, and photo/media-library access when you choose a profile picture, select media to share, or save media. Notifications are optional and require your permission. You can manage permissions in your device settings. The current app uses username-based contact adding; it does not need to read your phone's address book, and it does not request precise location access.

3. How we use information

  • To create and authenticate accounts and maintain profiles.
  • To find Guftin users by username and provide direct chats, groups, statuses, and calls.
  • To store, deliver, and synchronize messages and media across signed-in devices.
  • To deliver push notifications you enable.
  • To display and measure advertisements, maintain the Service, troubleshoot errors, protect users, and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. Who can see content you share

Your profile details may be shown to other Guftin users so they can identify you. Direct messages and files are intended for the conversation participants. Group messages and shared files may be seen by group members. Status posts are made available to your Guftin contacts and are designed to expire after 24 hours; related views and likes may also be visible in the app. Please check recipients before sharing sensitive information.

5. Encryption and security

Some supported message content and media are encrypted on your device before upload. However, Guftin does not currently provide complete end-to-end encryption across every feature and data type. Account details, contact relationships, group information, status interactions, notification data, and call/session information may still be processed by Guftin and its service providers as needed to operate the Service. Do not treat every feature as end-to-end encrypted. We use technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Service providers and sharing

We use third-party providers to operate parts of Guftin. These may include:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database, file storage, and real-time app services.
  • Google: Google sign-in and, where enabled, Google Mobile Ads services.
  • LiveKit: real-time voice/video call connectivity.
  • Expo and platform push services: app notifications and delivery infrastructure.

These providers process information according to their own privacy terms and the services they supply. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the Service, or address security and abuse issues. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Advertising

Guftin may display ads using Google Mobile Ads. The advertising provider may process device identifiers, app information, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and protect advertising, subject to its own policies and the settings available on your device. You can review Google's information about how it uses data at Google's advertising policies.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep account and service information for as long as needed to provide Guftin, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Some information may remain in protected backups or provider systems for a limited period after deletion. You can request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information by emailing uu786uu786uu@gmail.com from your registered email address. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Your choices

You can choose what profile information and content to share, whom to add, which permissions to grant, and whether to enable push notifications. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and verification.

10. Children and younger users

Guftin is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request. Users who are minors should use the Service with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian where required by local law.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Privacy protections in those countries may differ from those in your location.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Guftin changes. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of the Service after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, subject to applicable law.
