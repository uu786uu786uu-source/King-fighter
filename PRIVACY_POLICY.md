# Guftin Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2 November 2026**

Guftin is a messaging app that lets people create an account, set a username/profile, find and add contacts by username, exchange messages and media, share status updates, and use supported voice/video calling features.

This policy explains what information Guftin processes, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information Guftin processes

Depending on the features you use, Guftin may process:

- **Account information:** email address and authentication details. If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares the basic account information authorised on the Google consent screen, such as your name, email address and profile image.
- **Profile information:** Guftin username/ID, display name, profile photo and optional “about” information.
- **Contacts and conversations:** usernames you search for or add, contact relationships, conversation membership, messages, and delivery/read or activity information needed for chat features.
- **Shared content:** photos, videos, voice messages, files, status posts, and related timestamps, reactions, views or likes that you choose to share.
- **Calls:** technical session and connection information needed to establish supported voice/video calls. Call media is processed by the calling infrastructure to connect participants; this policy does not promise that calls are recorded or stored by Guftin.
- **Device and notification information:** push-notification tokens and basic technical information needed to deliver notifications and operate the app.
- **Advertising information:** if ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob may process advertising and device information under Google's own policies and settings.

Guftin's contact search is based on usernames/IDs entered in the app. The app is not intended to upload or synchronise your phone's address book.

## 2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, show profiles, find contacts, deliver messages and shared content, support status and calling features, send notifications, prevent abuse, maintain service reliability, and display ads where enabled.

## 3. Service providers

Guftin uses third-party infrastructure to operate its features. This includes:

- **Supabase** for authentication, database, file storage, real-time updates and backend functions.
- **Google** when a user chooses Google sign-in.
- **LiveKit** for supported real-time voice/video calling.
- **Expo notification services** and platform push services to deliver notifications.
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** where advertising is enabled.

These providers process relevant information to provide their services and are also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. Storage, security and encryption

Guftin uses access controls and security measures intended to protect account information and user content. The app includes device-side encryption features for supported message and media flows. This should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every feature or every category of information—including profile data, contact relationships, status metadata, push notifications, call signalling or service metadata—is end-to-end encrypted. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should keep their login credentials and one-time verification codes private.

## 5. Sharing

Guftin does not offer user information for sale. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above, disclosed where required by law, or used where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or its rights. Content you choose to send or post may be visible to the recipients or audience you select.

## 6. Retention and deletion requests

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods can differ by data type and provider.

To ask a question about your data or request account/data deletion, contact the developer through the [Guftin repository contact page](https://github.com/uu786uu786uu-source/King-fighter/issues). Do not post passwords, verification codes, private messages, identity documents or other sensitive personal information in a public issue. Ask for a private contact method instead.

## 7. Children's privacy

Guftin is not designed to encourage children to share sensitive personal information. Users should follow the age requirements that apply in their country and should seek help from a parent or guardian when needed.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Guftin's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this same URL, with its updated effective date.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the [Guftin repository contact page](https://github.com/uu786uu786uu-source/King-fighter/issues). Please do not include sensitive personal information in public posts.
